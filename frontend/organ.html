STATUTORY NOTICE • How Organ Donation Works

Unlike voluntary whole blood donation, organ and tissue matching is an intensely regulated medical, legal, and ethical process governed strictly by authorized transplant authorities including NOTTO (National Organ & Tissue Transplant Organisation • Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, India) and TNOS / TRANSTAN (Tamil Nadu Organ Sharing • Zonal Transplant Coordination Centre).

Life-Link does NOT: match patients with organ donors, maintain an independent organ waitlist, calculate distance-based organ allocation, or replace authorized government transplant coordination systems. All enquiries submitted here are recorded purely for informational routing to verified statutory authorities.

Types of Organ Donation

Deceased Donation (Cadaveric):
Organs are retrieved after certified Brain Stem Death (BSD). Mandatory family consent is verified. Managed exclusively by NOTTO and state ZTCC networks.

Living Donation:
A living person can donate one kidney or a segment of their liver, primarily to immediate family members, under rigorous ethics committee authorization.

✅

Who Can Pledge to Donate?

Any individual aged 18 or above can register an organ donor pledge regardless of chronic conditions. Suitability is evaluated medically at the exact time of donation by authorized transplant teams.

Pledging is an expression of intent — your family should be informed so they can honor your wishes when the time arrives.

🔍

Common Myths • Debunked

Myth: Emergency doctors won't prioritize saving me if I'm a pledged donor.
Fact: Emergency teams are entirely separate from transplant teams; patient care is always the absolute priority.

Myth: Organ retrieval disfigures the body.
Fact: Retrieval is performed with sterile surgical precision with utmost dignity and care.

🧠

Understanding Brain Stem Death

Brain Stem Death (BSD) signifies the permanent, irreversible cessation of all brain stem functions. Certified by a panel of four independent medical experts as mandated by the Transplantation of Human Organs and Tissues Act (THOTA), 1994.

📝 How to Pledge as an Organ Donor in India

  • 1
    Visit the NOTTO National Portal — Navigate to notto.mohfw.gov.in (National Organ & Tissue Transplant Organisation • Government of India).
  • 2
    Complete the Statutory Pledge Form — Register your details and choose the organs/tissues you wish to pledge.
  • 3
    Obtain Your Official Donor Card — Download, print, and keep your official government donor card with your emergency identification.
  • 4
    Inform Your Family — The essential step: discuss your choice with immediate family members since statutory consent is legally required.
  • 5
    Tamil Nadu Residents — Also register with tnos.org (TRANSTAN / ZTCC Tamil Nadu).
