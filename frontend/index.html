How Life-Link Coordinates Emergencies

An automated end-to-end pipeline connecting natural-language distress calls with verified nearby donors.

🗣️

1. AI Emergency Understanding

Patients or healthcare staff describe emergencies in natural English, Tamil, or Tanglish. The AI extracts hospital, blood group, component, units, and urgency level with clinical guardrails.

🎯

2. Intelligent Donor Matching

The matching engine evaluates biological ABO/Rh compatibility, verified readiness, and precise geodesic distances via Haversine algorithms to instantly rank the closest suitable voluntary donors.

📡

3. Adaptive Emergency Cascade

Search perimeters expand dynamically (0–5 km immediate vicinity → 5–15 km extended city → 15–25 km metro zone) until required units are fulfilled, with automated regional blood bank escalation.

⚠️
Medical Safety & Prototype Disclaimer: Life-Link is an emergency medical resource coordination platform designed to accelerate communication and resource discovery. It does not replace professional medical advice, clinical blood-bank cross-matching, or authorized organ allocation authorities.
